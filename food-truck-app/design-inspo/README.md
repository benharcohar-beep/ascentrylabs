## Shared style rules to carry forward

- Mostly white or off-white, near-black text, one accent colour at a time.
- One big number per screen or card, with a small comparison underneath ("+12% vs last week").
- Rounded cards with soft shadows, lots of white space, minimal grid lines on charts.
- Simple bar and line charts only, with the current period highlighted and the rest in grey.
- Floating or pill-shaped bottom nav with 3 to 4 icons.
- Plain-English labels, no finance jargon.

## How it maps to the app

- **Home:** today's/this week's revenue as the hero number, % vs last week, range pills, three tiles (orders, average order, best item).
- **Locations:** revenue per hour by spot, as a bar card with the best spot highlighted.
- **Menu:** top items list in the Lume "Top categories" style, with margin %.
- **Market:** price range bar with a marker for where your price sits (the sleep-quality bar from shots 03/04).
- **Tips:** weekly AI summary cards in the "Advises" style.
